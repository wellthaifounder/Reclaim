# Reclaim — Logo Design Brief

> Condensed brief for pasting into AI tools (Recraft, Midjourney, Ideogram) or for handing
> to a designer (Dribbble, 99designs). Last updated 2026-06-10.

## What Reclaim is

Reclaim (`reclaim.health`) is a custodian-agnostic HSA tool that **finds unclaimed healthcare
reimbursements** and generates **IRS-ready Substantiation Records** — a confirmation-timestamp audit
trail that is the product's defensible moat. Public launch: Sept 1 2026. (Rebrand from "Wellth.ai".)

## The core tension the mark must hold

The logo has to carry two feelings at once. A concept that only does one is incomplete:

1. **Empowerment** — _"reclaim what's yours."_ Money you didn't know you could get back. Motion of **return / recovery**.
2. **Officialdom & trust** — IRS-ready, audit-grade, proof. Healthcare + financial. Quiet, credible.

## Personality

- Confident and **calm**, not playful or cute.
- "Quiet competence." Reference set: **Wise, Mercury, Ramp, Stripe** — trust-fintech, not wellness-pastel.
- Geometric, precise, timeless over trendy.

## Hard constraints

- Must read at **16px** (favicon).
- Must work in **single color** (it gets stamped onto the Substantiation Record PDF).
- Needs a horizontal **wordmark lockup** and a standalone **icon**.
- Pairs with the `.health` TLD — consider a `Reclaim.health` lockup.

## Avoid (over-used / cheap in health-fintech)

Recycling arrows, medical crosses, heartbeat/EKG lines, dollar signs, piggy banks, coins with $, generic shields.

## Color (continuity from current palette — strong starting point)

| Role                | Value                          | Reads as                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Primary — deep teal | `#0e9b94` (`hsl(178 85% 36%)`) | health, calm, clinical trust |
| Accent — warm gold  | `#e0b03f` (`hsl(43 75% 58%)`)  | money, value, optimism       |
| Ink — navy          | `#1a2238` (`hsl(228 36% 16%)`) | authority, officialdom       |

Open to evolution, but teal + gold + navy already encodes health + money + trust cleanly.

## Concept directions (see `concepts/`)

- **A — Capture Brackets + Return:** scanner reticle (the "Capture" step) + upward return stroke. Most product-specific.
- **B — Return Orbit:** coin returning to center along an open orbit. Money coming back.
- **C — Substantiation Seal:** geometric R + official seal/checkmark. Strongest trust read; doubles as the PDF stamp.
- **D — Upward Recovery Stroke:** single stroke = checkmark = recovery. Most minimal / favicon-safe.

## Acceptance test

For every candidate: _Does it carry **both** empowerment and officialdom?_ If it only feels like
one, set it aside.
